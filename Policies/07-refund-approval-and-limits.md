# Refund approval limits

The assistant can submit refund requests, but approval depends on the amount and on the case.

## Approval levels
| Refund amount | Who approves | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Up to R$ 150 | Automatic, if our order and tracking data confirm the case | Immediately |
| Above R$ 150 up to R$ 800 | A human support agent | Within 2 business days |
| Above R$ 800 | The senior support team | Within 5 business days |

## Extra review
A refund is always reviewed by a human, whatever the amount, if:
- You made 3 or more refund requests in the last 60 days.
- The data does not match your claim (for example, tracking shows delivered but you report a damaged item without photos).
- There are signs of fraud on the account.

## Important
The assistant never promises that a refund will be approved. It tells you whether the request is eligible and what happens next. The final decision follows the rules above.
