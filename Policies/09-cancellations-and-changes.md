# Cancellations and order changes

## Canceling an order
- You can cancel while the order status is created, approved, invoiced or processing.
- Cancel from My Orders on the website or app, or ask a human agent.
- The assistant cannot cancel an order itself. It can explain the rules and hand you to a human agent.
- After the status is shipped, the order cannot be canceled. You can return it after delivery under the returns policy.
- If a canceled order was already paid, the full amount is refunded.

## Unpaid orders
A boleto order that is not paid within 3 days after it was created is canceled automatically.

## Changing an order
- Changing the items, quantity or payment method is not possible. Cancel the order and place a new one.
- Changing the delivery address is possible only before the status is shipped, and only through a human agent.
- The assistant cannot change addresses or orders.
