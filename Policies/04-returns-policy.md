# Returns policy

## Return window
You can return most items within 30 calendar days after the delivery date shown in your order.

## Conditions for a return
- The item is unused and in its original packaging, with all tags and accessories.
- You have not removed or damaged the product labels.

## Items that cannot be returned for change of mind
- Perishable food and drinks.
- Underwear, swimwear and personal hygiene items once opened or unsealed.
- Gift cards and digital products.
- Items made or personalized for you.

These items can still be returned if they arrive defective, damaged or different from the description.

## Return shipping cost
- Defective, damaged or wrong item: return shipping is free. We provide the label.
- Change of mind: a return label costs a flat R$ 15, deducted from your refund. You can also send the item back yourself at your own cost.

## How to start a return
Ask the assistant to start a return for the item. After the refund eligibility check, you receive a return label by email. Send the package within 7 calendar days after you receive the label.
