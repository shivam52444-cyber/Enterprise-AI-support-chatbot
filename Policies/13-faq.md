# Frequently asked questions

**Where is my order?**
The assistant checks the status of your order and shows the latest tracking information.

**How many days will my delivery take?**
It depends on the distance between you and the seller. The estimated date is shown at checkout. Typical times are 3 to 18 business days. See the delivery document.

**My order is late. What should I do?**
Wait until 2 business days after the estimated date. After that, you can open a delay case. After 10 calendar days, you can cancel for a full refund.

**Can the assistant place an order for me?**
No. Please use the KartNest website or app to place orders.

**Can I return an item if I just changed my mind?**
Yes, within 30 days after delivery, if it is unused and in its original packaging. A R$ 15 return label fee applies if you use our label.

**How long will my refund take?**
Credit card 5 to 10 business days, debit card 3 to 7, boleto 5 to 8 after bank details, voucher immediately. This counts from approval.

**Will I always get the refund I ask for?**
Only if the case meets the refund policy. Large refunds are reviewed by a human agent.

**I received a damaged item.**
Report it within 7 days of delivery with photos. Return shipping is free.

**Can I cancel my order?**
Yes, before it is shipped. The assistant cannot cancel it, but a human agent or My Orders can.
