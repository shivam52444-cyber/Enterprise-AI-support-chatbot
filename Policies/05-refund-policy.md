# Refund policy

## When you are eligible for a refund
1. You returned the item within the 30-day return window and we received and inspected it.
2. The item arrived defective, damaged or different from the description (reported within 7 days of delivery).
3. The order was never delivered and the carrier investigation confirmed it is lost.
4. The order status is unavailable or canceled and you already paid.
5. The order was delivered more than 10 calendar days after the estimated date and you cancel before delivery (see delivery document).

## How much you get back
- Defective, damaged, wrong, lost or undelivered item: the item price plus the original shipping fee.
- Change of mind: the item price only. The shipping fee is not refunded. If you used our return label, R$ 15 is deducted.
- Partly missing contents: the value of the missing items.
- Unavailable or canceled before shipping: everything you paid.

## When you are not eligible
- The return window of 30 days has passed and the item is not defective.
- The item is in the non-returnable list and there is no defect.
- The item was returned used, damaged by the customer, or without its accessories.
- The refund was already issued for the same item.

Only one open refund request is allowed per order item.
