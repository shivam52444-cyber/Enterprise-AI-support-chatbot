# Refund process and timelines

## Steps
1. You request a refund through the assistant or a human agent.
2. If a return is needed, you send the item back using the return label.
3. We receive and inspect the item. This takes 2 to 3 business days after it arrives.
4. The refund is approved and sent to your original payment method.

For items that do not need a return (for example unavailable or lost orders), the refund starts right after approval.

## How long the money takes to arrive
After approval:

| Original payment method | Time to receive the refund |
|---|---|
| Credit card | 5 to 10 business days. It can appear on your next statement. |
| Debit card | 3 to 7 business days. |
| Boleto | 5 to 8 business days after you send us your bank account details. |
| Voucher | Immediately, as voucher balance. |

## Orders paid in more than one way
If you paid with more than one method, each part is refunded to the method used for that part.

## Credit card installments
If you paid in installments, the refund follows the same installments. The bank decides how it appears on your statement.
