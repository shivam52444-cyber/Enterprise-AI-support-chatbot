# Human handoff and contact

## The assistant hands your case to a human agent when:
- A refund is above R$ 150, or needs human review (see the refund approval document).
- You ask to speak to a person.
- You want to cancel an order or change an address.
- You report a duplicate charge, suspected fraud, or an account problem.
- You have a legal complaint, or ask for a data request.
- The assistant cannot find the information or a system it needs is not working.
- The assistant fails to solve your problem after two tries.

## Human support hours
Monday to Saturday, 09:00 to 18:00 (Brasilia time). Outside these hours, the case is saved and an agent replies within 1 business day.

## What happens at handoff
The assistant creates a ticket with a summary of your problem and your order number, so you do not need to repeat it. You receive a ticket number.
