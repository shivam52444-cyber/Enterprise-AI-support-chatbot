# Damaged, wrong or missing items

## Damaged, defective or wrong item
- Report it within 7 calendar days after delivery.
- Send clear photos of the item, the label and the packaging.
- If the claim is accepted, you can choose a refund or a replacement from the seller. Return shipping is free.

## Missing items in a package
- Report it within 7 calendar days after delivery with a photo of the package and what is inside.
- You are refunded the value of the missing items.

## Order marked delivered but you did not receive it
1. Wait 2 business days. Check with neighbors or your building reception.
2. If it still has not arrived, you can open a claim within 15 calendar days after the delivered date.
3. We investigate with the carrier. This takes up to 7 business days.
4. If the package is confirmed lost, you get a full refund including shipping, or a replacement.

## What happens after 7 or 15 days
Claims made after these deadlines are not accepted automatically. A human agent can review them in special cases.
