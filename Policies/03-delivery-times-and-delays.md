# Delivery times and delayed orders

## How delivery time is calculated
Every order shows an estimated delivery date at checkout. It includes the seller's preparation time plus the carrier's transit time.

- Sellers must hand the package to the carrier within 3 business days after payment is approved.
- Typical total delivery time after payment approval, in business days:
  - Same state as the seller: 3 to 7 business days.
  - South and Southeast regions: 7 to 12 business days.
  - North and Northeast regions: 10 to 18 business days.
- Weekends and national holidays are not counted as business days.

## When is an order "late"?
An order is late when it has not been delivered by the estimated delivery date.

## What you can do if your order is late
1. Within 2 business days after the estimated date: please wait. Most delayed packages arrive in this time.
2. More than 2 business days after the estimated date: you can ask the assistant to open a delay case, and we will contact the seller and carrier.
3. More than 10 calendar days after the estimated date and still not delivered: you can choose to cancel the order and receive a full refund, including shipping.

## Compensation for late deliveries
If an order is delivered more than 7 calendar days after the estimated date, you receive a R$ 20 voucher. The voucher is valid for 90 days. The voucher is added automatically within 3 business days after delivery.
