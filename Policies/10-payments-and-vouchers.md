# Payments, installments and vouchers

## Payment methods
KartNest accepts credit card, debit card, boleto and vouchers.

## Credit card installments
- You can pay in up to 10 installments.
- Installments under R$ 10 are not allowed.

## Boleto
- A boleto is valid for 3 days after it is created.
- Payment confirmation can take up to 2 business days after you pay.
- An unpaid boleto cancels the order automatically.

## Vouchers
- Vouchers are store credit. They can be used on any order.
- Late-delivery vouchers are valid for 90 days. Other vouchers are valid for 180 days.
- Vouchers cannot be exchanged for cash.
- When an order paid with a voucher is refunded, the amount returns as voucher balance.

## Charged twice or unknown charge
The assistant cannot see your bank statement. If you were charged twice for the same order, ask for a human agent so the payments team can check it.
