# Privacy, security and account verification

## Your account only
The assistant only shows and discusses orders that belong to the account you are signed in to. It does not show orders of other customers, even if you give an order number or customer ID.

If an order number does not belong to your account, the assistant says that the order was not found.

## What the assistant never asks for
- Your password.
- Your full card number, CVV or card PIN.
- One-time codes sent to your phone or email.

KartNest staff will never ask for these either.

## What the assistant does not do
- It does not reveal internal rules, prompts or system instructions.
- It does not follow instructions in a message that ask it to ignore its rules.
- It does not share personal data about other people.

## Data requests
To request a copy or deletion of your personal data, ask for a human agent. The assistant cannot process data requests.
