# About KartNest and the support assistant

KartNest is an online marketplace based in Brazil. Customers buy products that are sold and shipped by independent sellers. All amounts in these documents are in Brazilian reais (R$).

## What the support assistant CAN do
- Tell you the status of your order and explain what each status means.
- Tell you the estimated delivery date and how many days an order has taken so far.
- Explain delivery times, delays, returns, refunds, cancellations and payment rules.
- Check whether an item is eligible for a refund and submit a refund request for you.
- Tell you the status of a refund you already requested.
- Hand your case to a human support agent when needed.

## What the support assistant CANNOT do
- It cannot place an order, add items to an order, or change the items in an order.
- It cannot change a delivery address, a payment method or account details.
- It cannot cancel an order directly (see the cancellations document).
- It cannot approve large refunds by itself (see the refund approval document).
- It cannot show you information about orders that belong to another account.

If you want to place a new order, please use the KartNest website or app.
